Get the Windows app

Transparent, always on top, and easy to move around.

Download for Windows
  1. If an older Scorebug is running, right-click its tray icon and choose Quit Scorebug.
  2. Run the downloaded Scorebug-Setup-Windows.exe.
  3. Open Scorebug. Your saved game, size, and position carry over.

Install it once. Future updates download in the background and finish when you quit or choose Restart to update. Scorebug won’t restart in the middle of a game.

Prefer a portable app? Download the ZIP, extract it, and open the Scorebug-Portable application. Portable copies need manual updates.

Download not starting? Open the latest release, find Assets, and choose Scorebug-Setup-Windows.exe.

Good to know

Do the scores update by themselves?

Yes. Scores and stats check about every 2 seconds while your computer is awake and online. Slow or failed feeds trigger a backup. If every feed is down, the last score stays visible and is marked as delayed. The clock shows the latest reported time, so the provider can still lag behind the game or your TV. Browser tabs may slow down in the background; the Windows app keeps checking.

Which floating version should I use?

The Windows app gives you a transparent scorebug that snaps to the sides. The browser version keeps a browser frame and needs its original tab to stay open. You can also pin Clean view with PowerToys using Win + Ctrl + T.

What do the animations mean?

New scores and confirmed interceptions or fumble turnovers get a short banner. A big play is a run of at least 20 yards or a catch of at least 25 yards. Banners show the player’s name and photo when available, then animate away. Opening a game won’t replay old plays. Use the demo controls above to try each animation.
