      Get the desktop app

      Transparent, always on top, and easy to move around.

      Download for Windows
      1. If an older Scorebug is running, right-click its tray icon and choose Quit Scorebug.
      2. Run the downloaded Scorebug-Setup-Windows.exe.
      3. Open Scorebug. Your saved game, size, and position carry over.

      Install it once. Future updates download in the background and finish when you quit or choose Restart to update. Scorebug won’t restart in the middle of a game.

      Prefer a portable app? Download the ZIP, extract it, and open the Scorebug-Portable application. Portable copies need manual updates.

      Mac · macOS 13 or newer

      Mac · Apple siliconMac · Intel

      Open the disk image and drag Scorebug into Applications. Check Apple menu → About This Mac if you’re unsure which download to use. A Mac with an M-series chip uses Apple silicon.

      This first Mac release is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway only if you trust this download. Mac updates are installed manually from these links.

      Download not starting? Open the latest release, find Assets, and choose the Windows installer or the Mac download for your processor.

      Good to know

      Do the scores update by themselves?

      Yes. Selected games check about every 2 seconds; other leagues containing your favorites check about every 5 seconds. Your computer needs to stay awake and online. Slow or failed feeds trigger the available backups. If every feed is down, the last score stays visible and is marked as delayed. The clock shows the latest reported time, so the provider can still lag behind the game or your TV. Browser tabs may slow down in the background; the desktop app keeps checking. OpenF1 timing and provider limits have their own update cadence.

      Which floating version should I use?

      The Windows and Mac apps give you a transparent scorebug that snaps to the sides. The browser version keeps a browser frame and needs its original tab to stay open. You can also pin Clean view with PowerToys using Win + Ctrl + T.

      What do the animations mean?

      Each sport has its own events: touchdowns and turnovers, three-pointers and lead changes, home runs, goals, or race-control flags. A banner needs a newly reported event; the app does not invent a play from a score alone. Player names and pictures appear when the feed supplies them. Opening a game does not replay old events. Try the demo controls to see the animations.

      How does Formula 1 coverage work?

      The public schedule includes practice, qualifying, sprints when scheduled, and races. Public timing can be delayed and may omit gaps, tyres or race-control flags. A configured OpenF1 account can supply richer live timing; genuine live access requires a paid OpenF1 subscription. The app labels the source and never fills missing timing with made-up values.
