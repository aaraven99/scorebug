A few things to know +

Live, without reloading. The app checks ESPN’s scoreboard feed every 10 seconds and updates the strip in place. The game clock shows the feed’s last reported value; it doesn’t count down between updates.

Desktop mode. Download the Windows artifact from the latest successful desktop build on GitHub. Extract it, then open the portable app. It runs on your computer and does not need a Vercel deployment. Side positions turn vertical automatically. Hover over the scorebug for field position, the latest play, and team stats. Use its tray menu for positioning.

Browser mode. Chrome and Edge can open an always-on-top Picture-in-Picture window. The browser keeps its own frame, and you drag it into position. If that feature isn’t available, open Clean view and use PowerToys: Win + Ctrl + Shift + T to crop, or Win + Ctrl + T to pin the window.

Feed interruptions. Last known scores stay visible with a “feed delayed” label while the app reconnects. A sleeping computer or discarded browser tab cannot keep fetching. Broadcast and feed timing can differ.
